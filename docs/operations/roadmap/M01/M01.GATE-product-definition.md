## Residual Risk Decision

candidate and M12 must regenerate all artifact, permission, privacy, and Data Safety facts.

Existing dependency expiry, security-alert reconciliation, isolated staging, continuity, and other
M00 risks remain open. These risks do not make the product definition ambiguous, so `PROD-01`
passes. They explicitly stop their downstream evidence claims when the affected gate is reached.
