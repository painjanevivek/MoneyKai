## Scope

Four canonical templates were added under `docs/governance/templates/`:

- ADRs compare options and record mandatory user, financial, security, privacy,
  reliability, accessibility, rollout, and rollback impact.
- Risk registers retain cause/event/consequence, ratings, controls, owners, due dates,
  triggers, evidence, acceptance, and closure history.
- Evidence records bind exact procedures and results to immutable revisions, builds,
  environments, devices, artifacts, findings, exceptions, and decisions.
- Risk acceptances are narrowly scoped, explicitly approved, monitored, automatically
  expiring, and unable to waive the constitution's prohibited risks.
